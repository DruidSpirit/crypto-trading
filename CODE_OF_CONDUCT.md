# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors who communicate clearly, respectfully, and
constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and patience.
- Keep reviews focused on code, documentation, tests, and project outcomes.
- Be considerate of contributors with different experience levels and
  backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private messages, credentials, account data, or trading data
  without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block contributors whose
behavior harms the project or its contributors.
